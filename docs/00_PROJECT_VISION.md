# Ἀνάγνωσις — Project Vision

## Purpose

Ἀνάγνωσις exists to cultivate a lifelong rhythm of reading the Greek Scriptures.

It answers one question:

> What should I read today?

The application removes decision fatigue and quietly guides the reader through a daily pattern of prayer, familiar reading, progressive reading, and meditation.

## Identity

Ἀνάγνωσις is not a general Bible application, study platform, grammar tool, or digital library.

It is a daily Greek reading office.

The application should feel like opening a prayer book to the appointed reading rather than opening software.

## Relationship to Other Projects

* Living in Koine is a world.
* BLC Companion is a course.
* Ἀνάγνωσις is a habit.

Each project has a distinct purpose and must not absorb the responsibilities of the others.

## Guiding Principle

Every proposed feature must answer:

> Does this help the reader hear and receive God’s Word in Greek today?

Features that do not directly support that purpose do not belong.

## Core Experience

The application opens directly to today’s reading.

There is no dashboard, welcome page, content feed, or discovery screen.

The intended rhythm is:

1. Open.
2. Pray.
3. Read.
4. Complete the office.
5. Leave.

## Daily Rhythm

Each day follows the same general pattern:

1. Opening prayer
2. Familiar reading
3. Progressive reading
4. Psalm or biblical prayer
5. Closing prayer

## Reading Journeys

### Familiar Reading

Large portions of previously encountered or accessible narrative text.

Purpose:

* reading fluency
* comfort
* speed
* direct comprehension
* sustained narrative attention

Likely books include Mark, John, and later reread cycles.

### Progressive Reading

Steady movement through a current biblical book.

Initial sequence:

* Luke
* Acts
* Genesis
* Matthew
* selected letters

Purpose:

* continued growth
* exposure to unfamiliar text
* sustained book-level reading

### Prayer Cycle

Small Psalms, canticles, and biblical prayers.

Purpose:

* slow reading
* meditation
* prayer in Greek

## Reading Philosophy

Reading units should follow discourse and narrative structure rather than arbitrary verse quotas.

A coherent scene should remain intact whenever practical.

Reading plans should allow books to be completed with meaningful momentum rather than stretched across unnecessarily long periods.

## Platform

Version 1 is:

* iPhone-oriented
* portrait-first
* offline-first
* installable as a Progressive Web App
* local-only
* account-free
* server-free

## Progress Philosophy

Progress should communicate continuity without becoming a source of pressure.

The application must not include:

* streaks
* scores
* achievements
* trophies
* competitive statistics
* confetti
* guilt-based reminders

Progress may include:

* current journey
* current book position
* completed books
* quiet reading calendar
* journey history

## Completion

When the daily office is complete, the primary message is:

> Εἰρήνη σοι.

The application should then become quiet.

## Version 1 Included Scope

* Today view
* daily prayer and reading sequence
* discourse-aware reading plans
* reading view
* journey progress
* reading calendar
* local completion state
* light mode
* dark mode
* English assistance toggle
* offline PWA support

## Explicitly Excluded

* highlighting
* notes
* morphology
* dictionaries
* search
* verse comparison
* sharing
* accounts
* cloud synchronization
* social features
* audio
* AI features
* achievements
* gamification

## Product Character

The application should be:

* quiet
* restrained
* reverent
* readable
* dependable
* immediate
* beautiful without ornamentation

Its success is measured not by feature count or engagement time, but by whether it helps establish a stable rhythm of reading Greek Scripture.
